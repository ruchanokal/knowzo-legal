# Privacy Policy

## 1. General
This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is collected, processed and stored in connection with the Knowzo mobile application (the "App"), operated by Ruchan Okal. By using the App you accept the terms of this policy.


2. Information Collected

2.1 Account Information
When the App is opened, an anonymous session is created via Firebase Authentication; no name, email or phone number is requested. The system generates a random user identifier (UID).

The optional display name and country you set for the leaderboard are stored on our servers.

2.2 Gameplay Data
Questions answered, correctness and response time per question
Total points, lifetime accuracy, current streak and daily-quest progress
Selected app language
Computed global leaderboard rank

2.3 Automatically Collected Information
Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
App version, crash reports, session data
Approximate location (country derived from IP; the IP address itself is not retained long-term)
A randomly generated installation ID
In-app event data (game start/end, question shown, answer result, ad request/impression)

2.4 Advertising Information
Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android), the ads you see and your interactions with them may be collected by Google AdMob. If App Tracking Transparency is declined on iOS, only non-personalized ads are shown.


3. Use of Information
Collected information is used solely for the following purposes:

Operating the App (loading questions, validating answers, saving scores)
Showing advertisements to keep the App free of charge
Improving service quality and analyzing product usage
Detecting and fixing crashes and bugs
Preventing abuse of the leaderboard


4. Information Security
Appropriate technical and organizational measures are in place to protect your personal data. Server-side validation, Firestore security rules preventing cross-user data access and authenticated access mechanisms are used. However, no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.


5. Sharing with Third Parties
Your personal data is not shared with third parties except in the following situations:

Legal obligations (court order, prosecutor request, etc.)
Technical infrastructure providers necessary for service delivery

The following Google services process data on our behalf:

Firebase Authentication (anonymous user identifier)
Firebase Firestore (gameplay data and profile)
Firebase Cloud Functions (server-side answer validation)
Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)
Google Analytics for Firebase (usage analytics)
Firebase Remote Config (remote configuration)
Firebase Cloud Messaging (only if notifications are accepted)
Google AdMob (advertising)

Google's processing of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase Data Processing Terms.


6. Advertising Identifiers
Our mobile App does not use traditional browser cookies. Instead, the device's advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID) may be used by AdMob for ad-frequency limiting and ad personalization. You can reset this identifier or disable personalization at any time from your device settings.


7. Data Retention and Deletion
Gameplay data is retained for as long as the App remains installed
Analytics data is retained per Firebase's default retention settings (typically 14 months for event-level data)
Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days
Deletion requests may be sent to knowzo@yandex.com at any time and will be processed within 30 days


8. Children's Privacy
The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. AdMob settings are configured to deliver only ads suitable for general audiences.


9. User Rights
Under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and applicable EU GDPR provisions, you have the right to:

Access the personal data processed about you
Request a copy of that data
Request correction of inaccurate data
Request deletion or destruction of the data
Withdraw your consent to ad personalization

Requests may be sent to knowzo@yandex.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or your local supervisory authority.


10. International Data Transfers
User data is primarily stored in Google Cloud's europe-west1 (Belgium) region. Aggregate analytics data may be processed in other Google data centers worldwide.


11. Limitation of Liability
The App is not responsible for the content of third-party websites or advertisements linked from within
No liability is accepted for indirect damages arising from service interruptions or data loss
Advertising content is supplied by the AdMob network; the accuracy or appropriateness of individual ads is not the App's responsibility

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Turkey. The courts and enforcement offices of Istanbul shall have jurisdiction over any dispute arising hereunder.
